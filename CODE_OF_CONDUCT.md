# Code of conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). Everyone participating in issues, pull requests, and discussions is expected to follow it.

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainers through [GitHub private reporting](https://github.com/exmergo/github-repo-analytics/security/advisories/new) or by contacting a member of the [exmergo organization](https://github.com/exmergo). Reports are reviewed promptly and kept confidential. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behavior they deem harmful, following the Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
